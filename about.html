About Us

Our mission

Book Bridge is a college Community Service Project (CSP). We collect reusable books from donors and distribute them free of cost to students in need, reducing educational expenses and promoting knowledge sharing.

Our values

  • Free for everyone - no payments, ever.
  • Reuse - every book deserves a second life.
  • Fairness - requests are reviewed by administrators.
  • Community - students, donors and volunteers working together.
