How It Works

From a donor's shelf to a student's desk in three steps

1

Donate

Donors submit reusable books with title, author, category, education level, condition and a photo.

An administrator checks each donation before it is listed.

2

Connect

Students search and filter the available books and send a free request.

Track the request status from your dashboard.

3

Distribute

Approved books are handed over free of cost and recorded in the distribution records.

Students can then share feedback.
