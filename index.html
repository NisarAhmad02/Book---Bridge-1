About Book Bridge

Many students cannot afford the textbooks they need, while many homes have good books lying unused. Book Bridge connects the two: donors give reusable books, and students receive them completely free of cost. Less expense for students, more knowledge shared for everyone.

1

Donate

2

Connect

Students search and request available books.

3

Distribute

Approved books are distributed free of cost.
